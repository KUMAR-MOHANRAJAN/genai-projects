LEAVE POLICY — AUTORAG SOLUTIONS PTE LTD
Version 2.1 | Effective Date: 01 January 2026 | Owner: People Operations

1. SCOPE AND PURPOSE

This policy applies to all full-time permanent employees of AutoRAG Solutions. It defines entitlements, eligibility conditions, application procedures, and rules governing all leave types. This policy supersedes all previous leave policies.

2. ANNUAL LEAVE

2.1 Entitlement
Employees are entitled to paid annual leave based on years of continuous service:
  - Less than 3 years: 18 working days per year
  - 3 years to less than 7 years: 21 working days per year
  - 7 years and above: 25 working days per year

2.2 Accrual
Annual leave accrues at a rate of 1.5 days per completed month of service for employees entitled to 18 days, and proportionally higher for higher entitlements. Leave begins accruing from the first day of employment.

2.3 Carryover
Up to 5 unused annual leave days may be carried over to the following calendar year. Carried-over leave must be consumed by 31 March of the following year. Leave that is not consumed by this date is forfeited without compensation.

2.4 Encashment
Annual leave may be encashed only upon separation from the company. The encashment value is calculated using the employee's basic daily salary at the time of separation.

2.5 Application
Annual leave must be applied for at least 3 business days in advance through the HR portal for durations of 3 days or fewer. Leave of 4 or more consecutive days requires a minimum of 10 business days advance notice. Emergency annual leave applications may be approved at the manager's discretion.

3. SICK LEAVE

3.1 Entitlement
All full-time employees receive 12 days of paid sick leave per year. Sick leave does not accumulate or carry over between calendar years.

3.2 Medical Certificate
A medical certificate from a registered medical practitioner is required for absences of 2 or more consecutive days. The certificate must be submitted to People Operations within 3 business days of returning to work.

3.3 Extended Illness
Employees who exhaust their sick leave entitlement may apply to use annual leave for continued absence due to illness. If all leave entitlements are exhausted, the employee may apply for unpaid medical leave for up to 30 additional days, subject to management approval.

4. MATERNITY AND PATERNITY LEAVE

4.1 Maternity Leave
The primary caregiver is entitled to 26 weeks of paid maternity leave. This may commence up to 4 weeks before the expected delivery date. The leave is paid at 100% of basic salary. Employees must provide at least 6 weeks written notice and a medical certificate confirming the expected delivery date.

4.2 Paternity Leave
The secondary caregiver is entitled to 4 weeks of paid paternity leave to be taken within 16 weeks of the child's birth or adoption. This leave must be taken in a single continuous block unless otherwise agreed with the manager and People Operations.

4.3 Adoption Leave
Employees adopting a child under 12 months of age are entitled to the same leave as biological parents. The leave commences on the date of legal custody transfer.

5. BEREAVEMENT LEAVE

Employees are entitled to paid bereavement leave upon the death of a family member:
  - Spouse, child, parent, or sibling: 5 working days
  - Grandparent, parent-in-law, or sibling-in-law: 2 working days

Additional unpaid leave may be granted at the manager's discretion for travel requirements. Evidence of bereavement may be requested.

6. UNPAID LEAVE

Unpaid leave may be granted for personal circumstances not covered by other leave types. Applications must be submitted in writing to People Operations at least 2 weeks in advance. Approval is at the company's discretion and is not guaranteed. Unpaid leave does not affect annual leave accrual for periods of up to 30 days.

7. PUBLIC HOLIDAYS

Employees are entitled to all public holidays gazetted by the relevant government authority for their work location. If an employee is required to work on a public holiday, they will receive a substitute day off or additional compensation as required by local law.

8. LEAVE WITHOUT PAY (LWOP)

LWOP reduces the employee's monthly salary on a pro-rated daily basis. Continuous LWOP exceeding 30 days must be approved by the Chief People Officer. LWOP periods of more than 90 days may affect seniority calculations and benefit eligibility.
