LEAVE POLICY - NORTHSTAR CANADA
Version 1.0 | Effective Date: 01 September 2026 | Owner: People Operations

1. SCOPE AND PURPOSE

NorthStar Canada is a federally regulated employer. This policy sets company entitlements for Canadian employees; where the Canada Labour Code provides a greater minimum protection, the Code applies.

2. ANNUAL LEAVE

2.1 Entitlement
Employees are entitled to paid vacation based on years of continuous service:
  - Less than 5 years: 15 working days per year
  - 5 years or more: 20 working days per year

2.2 Accrual
Vacation accrues monthly from the first day of employment.

2.3 Carryover
Up to 5 unused vacation days may be carried over to the following calendar year. Carried-over days must be used by 31 March.

2.4 Encashment
Unused earned vacation is paid out on separation from the company.

2.5 Application
Annual leave must be applied for at least 3 business days in advance through the HR portal for durations of 3 days or fewer. Leave of 4 or more consecutive days requires a minimum of 10 business days advance notice. Emergency annual leave applications may be approved at the manager's discretion.

3. SICK LEAVE

3.1 Entitlement
Employees receive up to 10 paid medical-leave days per calendar year. After 30 days of continuous employment, employees earn 3 days, then 1 additional day per completed month, to a maximum of 10 days.

3.2 Medical Certificate
For an absence of 3 or more consecutive scheduled days, People Operations may request reasonable supporting documentation.

3.3 Extended Illness
Employees remain eligible for job-protected medical leave under the Canada Labour Code beyond the paid entitlement.

4. MATERNITY AND PATERNITY LEAVE

4.1 Maternity Leave
Eligible employees may take up to 17 weeks of unpaid maternity leave under the Canada Labour Code. NorthStar provides a supplementary benefit of up to 8 weeks at 75% of regular base pay for employees eligible for Employment Insurance maternity benefits.

4.2 Paternity Leave
Eligible natural or adoptive parents may take up to 63 weeks of unpaid parental leave. NorthStar provides 2 weeks of paid partner leave to the non-birthing parent.

4.3 Adoption Leave
Employees should provide at least 4 weeks' written notice where practicable. Employment and seniority continue during statutory leave.

5. BEREAVEMENT LEAVE

Employees receive 5 paid days for the death of an immediate family member. The federal minimum provides up to 10 days of bereavement leave, with the first 3 days paid after 3 consecutive months of employment.

6. UNPAID LEAVE

Unpaid leave may be granted for personal circumstances not covered by other leave types. Applications must be submitted in writing to People Operations at least 2 weeks in advance. Approval is at the company's discretion and is not guaranteed. Unpaid leave does not affect annual leave accrual for periods of up to 30 days.

7. PUBLIC HOLIDAYS

Employees are eligible for federal general holidays under the Statutory Holidays Policy. Employees required to work receive holiday pay and a substitute day off, subject to applicable rules.

8. LEAVE WITHOUT PAY (LWOP)

LWOP reduces the employee's monthly salary on a pro-rated daily basis. Continuous LWOP exceeding 30 days must be approved by the Chief People Officer. LWOP periods of more than 90 days may affect seniority calculations and benefit eligibility.
