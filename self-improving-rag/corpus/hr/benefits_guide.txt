EMPLOYEE BENEFITS GUIDE - NORTHSTAR CANADA
FY2026 | People Operations

OVERVIEW

NorthStar Canada provides benefits to regular full-time employees from their first day of employment. This guide summarizes company benefits and does not replace plan documents.

1. HEALTH AND WELLNESS

Group Medical Insurance
All employees receive extended health, prescription drug, dental, and vision coverage through the group plan. The annual dental maximum is CAD 1,500 and the vision maximum is CAD 400 every 24 months.

Eligible dependants may be enrolled during onboarding, annual open enrolment in November, or after a qualifying life event.

Mental Health Support
All employees have access to 6 free counselling sessions per year through our Employee Assistance Program (EAP) provider, Mindfi. Sessions are confidential and can be booked directly through the Mindfi app. Topics covered include stress, anxiety, relationship concerns, and career transitions.

Wellness Allowance
Employees receive a CAD 75 monthly wellness allowance for fitness, wellbeing, or ergonomic home-office expenses. Claims require a receipt and are submitted through the Finance portal.

2. RETIREMENT AND FINANCIAL BENEFITS

Registered Retirement Savings Plan (RRSP)
NorthStar matches employee RRSP contributions dollar for dollar up to 4% of base salary after 3 months of service. The company also makes required Canada Pension Plan and Employment Insurance contributions.

Annual Bonus
Eligible employees may receive an annual performance bonus paid in March. The target bonus is 10% of annual basic salary for employees rated Meets Expectations, up to 20% for Exceptional performers. Employees must be employed on the payout date to receive the bonus.

Employee Stock Option Plan (ESOP)
Senior employees (Band 6 and above) are eligible for stock options granted annually in June. Vesting occurs over 4 years with a 1-year cliff. Full details are provided in the ESOP agreement.

3. LEARNING AND DEVELOPMENT

Training Budget
Each employee receives a CAD 2,000 annual learning budget for role-relevant courses, certifications, conferences, or books. Claims must be submitted with receipts within 30 days.

Certification exam fees are reimbursed at 100% on the first pass and 50% on a re-sit, within the annual learning budget.

Study Leave
Employees pursuing role-relevant professional qualifications may apply for up to 5 days of paid study leave per year for exams. This is separate from annual leave.

4. FAMILY BENEFITS

Childcare Support
Employees with children under age 7 receive a CAD 150 monthly childcare subsidy per child, up to 2 children.

Parental Leave
Refer to the Leave Policy document for detailed maternity, paternity, and adoption leave entitlements.

5. WORK FLEXIBILITY

Flexible Work Arrangement
Eligible employees may adjust their start and end times by up to 2 hours from the standard schedule. Approval requires written agreement from the line manager and must be registered with People Operations.

Remote Work Equipment
Hybrid employees are eligible for a one-time CAD 600 home-office equipment allowance. IT approval is required before purchasing security-sensitive equipment.
