EMPLOYEE BENEFITS GUIDE — AUTORAG SOLUTIONS PTE LTD
FY2026 | People Operations

OVERVIEW

AutoRAG Solutions provides a comprehensive benefits package to all full-time permanent employees. Benefits are effective from the first day of employment unless otherwise stated. This guide summarizes available benefits; refer to the individual benefit plan documents for full terms and conditions.

1. HEALTH AND WELLNESS

Group Medical Insurance
All employees receive group medical coverage underwritten by Great Eastern Life. The plan covers:
- Inpatient hospitalization: SGD 100,000 per year
- Outpatient general practitioner: SGD 1,500 per year (SGD 50 co-pay per visit)
- Specialist consultation: SGD 3,000 per year (requires GP referral; 20% co-pay)
- Dental: SGD 800 per year (basic cleaning and fillings only)
- Optical: SGD 500 per year (lenses and frames)

Dependents (spouse and children under 21) may be enrolled at the employee's expense. The annual premium for one dependent is SGD 2,400; for a family plan (spouse plus children) it is SGD 4,800. Enrollment changes are permitted only during the annual open enrollment window in November.

Mental Health Support
All employees have access to 6 free counselling sessions per year through our Employee Assistance Program (EAP) provider, Mindfi. Sessions are confidential and can be booked directly through the Mindfi app. Topics covered include stress, anxiety, relationship concerns, and career transitions.

Wellness Allowance
Employees receive a monthly wellness allowance of SGD 80 credited to their CXA employee benefits wallet. This can be used for gym memberships, fitness classes, meditation apps, or ergonomic equipment for home offices.

2. RETIREMENT AND FINANCIAL BENEFITS

Central Provident Fund (Singapore)
The company contributes to CPF in full accordance with statutory requirements. Contribution rates follow the CPF Board schedule based on age and wage ceiling.

Provident Fund (India)
For employees based in India, the company contributes 12% of basic salary to the Employee Provident Fund (EPF) in addition to the statutory employer contribution.

Annual Bonus
Eligible employees may receive an annual performance bonus paid in March. The target bonus is 10% of annual basic salary for employees rated Meets Expectations, up to 20% for Exceptional performers. Employees must be employed on the payout date to receive the bonus.

Employee Stock Option Plan (ESOP)
Senior employees (Band 6 and above) are eligible for stock options granted annually in June. Vesting occurs over 4 years with a 1-year cliff. Full details are provided in the ESOP agreement.

3. LEARNING AND DEVELOPMENT

Training Budget
Each employee receives an annual L&D budget of SGD 2,500 (or INR 100,000 for India-based employees). This may be used for external courses, certifications, conferences, or books. Claims must be submitted through the Finance portal with receipts within 30 days of expenditure.

Approved certification reimbursements include: AWS certifications (up to SGD 800), Google Cloud certifications (up to SGD 800), professional certifications relevant to the employee's role. Certification exam fees are reimbursed at 100% upon first pass. Re-sit fees are reimbursed at 50%.

Study Leave
Employees pursuing role-relevant professional qualifications may apply for up to 5 days of paid study leave per year for exams. This is separate from annual leave.

4. FAMILY BENEFITS

Childcare Support
Employees with children below age 7 receive a monthly childcare subsidy of SGD 150 per child (maximum 2 children). The subsidy is paid directly to the employee's bank account on the last business day of each month.

Parental Leave
Refer to the Leave Policy document for detailed maternity, paternity, and adoption leave entitlements.

5. WORK FLEXIBILITY

Flexible Work Arrangement
Eligible employees may adjust their start and end times by up to 2 hours from the standard schedule. Approval requires written agreement from the line manager and must be registered with People Operations.

Remote Work Equipment
New employees working in a hybrid arrangement are eligible for a one-time home office equipment allowance of SGD 500. This may be used for a monitor, keyboard, mouse, or chair. Purchases must be approved in advance by the IT team and receipts submitted within 60 days.
