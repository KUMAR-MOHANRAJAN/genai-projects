STATUTORY HOLIDAYS POLICY - NORTHSTAR CANADA
Version 1.0 | Effective Date: 01 September 2026 | Owner: People Operations

NorthStar Canada is a federally regulated employer. This policy summarizes how federal general holidays are administered. It does not replace the Canada Labour Code or a collective agreement that provides a greater benefit.

Eligible employees receive paid time off for New Year's Day, Good Friday, Victoria Day, Canada Day, Labour Day, National Day for Truth and Reconciliation, Thanksgiving Day, Remembrance Day, Christmas Day, and Boxing Day. When a listed holiday falls on a weekend or another non-working day, People Operations will publish the observed day for the calendar year.

Employees scheduled to work a general holiday receive their regular wages for the hours worked plus holiday pay, and a substitute day off with pay. Managers must arrange the substitute day within the applicable scheduling period and record it in the HR system.

When a general holiday occurs during approved vacation, the employee is not required to use a vacation day for that holiday. The holiday is recorded separately and the vacation balance remains available.

Provincial employment standards can apply to employees who are not federally regulated. People Operations must confirm the employee's work jurisdiction before interpreting a provincial holiday entitlement.

Source reference: Canada Labour Code federal labour standards, general holidays.
