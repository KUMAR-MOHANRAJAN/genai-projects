EXPENSE REIMBURSEMENT POLICY
NorthStar Canada | Version 1.0 | Finance Department | Updated: September 2026

1. PURPOSE AND SCOPE

This policy governs the reimbursement of business expenses incurred by NorthStar Canada employees and contractors. All amounts are in Canadian dollars (CAD).

2. GENERAL PRINCIPLES

Expenses must be: (a) ordinary and necessary for legitimate business purposes, (b) pre-approved where required, (c) supported by original receipts, and (d) submitted within 30 days of incurrence. Expenses submitted after 60 days require Finance Director approval and may be declined.

Employees must not: split expenses to avoid approval thresholds, submit personal expenses as business expenses, or receive reimbursement for the same expense through multiple channels.

3. EXPENSE CATEGORIES AND LIMITS

3.1 Travel — Domestic (within country of employment)
  - Economy class air travel for flights under 4 hours: up to CAD 700 per journey
  - Taxi and ride-hailing: receipts required; no daily limit for business travel
  - Parking: receipts required; up to CAD 45 per day
  - Accommodation: up to CAD 275 per night in Canada
  - Meals during domestic travel: up to CAD 90 per day (three meals combined)

3.2 Travel — International
  - Economy class air travel: required for flights under 6 hours
  - Business class permitted for: flights over 6 hours, medical necessity (with documentation), or VP+ level with Director approval
  - Pre-approval required for all international travel via the Travel Request form (minimum 5 business days advance notice)
  - International accommodation: up to CAD 425 per night in high-cost cities; CAD 300 in other cities
  - Meals during international travel: up to CAD 125 per day
  - Travel insurance: booked through the company's corporate travel program; personal travel insurance is not reimbursable

3.3 Client Entertainment
  - Meals and entertainment with clients or prospects: up to CAD 175 per person per occasion
  - Events (tickets, activities): up to CAD 225 per person per event
  - Requires: prior manager approval, names and organizations of all attendees documented, clear business purpose stated
  - Alcohol at client meals: up to CAD 45 per person per occasion; reimbursable only when client is present
  - Gifts to clients: up to CAD 75 per gift; must comply with client organization's gift policies

3.4 Technology and Equipment
  - Work-from-home internet subsidy: CAD 60 per month (no receipt required; added to monthly payroll)
  - Mobile phone bill reimbursement for employees with mobile phone allowance: up to CAD 90 per month with itemized bill
  - Software subscriptions for business use: require IT approval before purchase; up to CAD 125 per month without Finance Director approval
  - Hardware (laptop peripherals, monitors): must be approved through IT; home office allowance CAD 600 (one-time, for hybrid workers)

3.5 Professional Development
  - Refer to the Benefits Guide for L&D budget limits (CAD 2,000 per year)
  - Conference registration and travel are charged against the L&D budget plus the travel budget where applicable
  - Professional membership fees: up to CAD 400 per year per membership; maximum 2 memberships reimbursable per employee per year

4. APPROVAL MATRIX

| Expense Amount | Approver Required |
|----------------|-------------------|
| Up to CAD 750 | Direct manager |
| CAD 751 to CAD 3,000 | Director |
| CAD 3,001 to CAD 12,000 | VP |
| Above CAD 12,000 | CFO |

International travel requires an additional Finance team approval regardless of amount.

5. SUBMISSION PROCEDURE

Expenses must be submitted through the Finance portal (finance.autorag-solutions.com/expenses):
1. Log in with corporate credentials
2. Select "New Expense Report"
3. Attach digital receipts (JPEG or PDF; maximum 5 MB per receipt)
4. Assign the correct cost centre code (obtain from your manager)
5. Submit for manager approval

Approved expense reports are paid in the next payroll cycle if submitted before the 20th of the month. Submissions after the 20th are paid in the following month's cycle.

6. NON-REIMBURSABLE EXPENSES

The following are not reimbursable under any circumstances:
  - Personal purchases, groceries, clothing, or personal care items
  - Traffic fines, parking tickets, or toll violations incurred during personal use
  - Alcohol purchased without a client present
  - Upgrades to business or first class without prior approval
  - Expenses related to personal social events (team celebrations not on the approved company calendar)
  - Gym memberships (covered by the separate wellness allowance in Benefits)
  - Losses or theft of personal items during travel
  - Currency conversion fees when converting to personal accounts
