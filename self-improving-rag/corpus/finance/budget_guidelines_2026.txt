BUDGET PLANNING GUIDELINES — FY2026
NorthStar Canada | Finance Department | Confidential | Updated: September 2026

1. OVERVIEW

This document provides guidelines for department heads preparing FY2026 budget submissions. All budget requests must be submitted through the Finance portal by 15 February 2026. Late submissions will be incorporated into a budget revision in Q2, which may result in delayed headcount approvals.

2. COMPANY FINANCIAL TARGETS FY2026

Revenue target: CAD 24 million
Gross margin target: 72%
Total headcount budget: 58 FTE (current: 34 FTE; net new: 24 positions)
Capital expenditure budget: CAD 1.5 million
Operating expenditure growth cap: 95% of revenue growth (for every CAD 1.00 of new revenue, departments may request up to CAD 0.95 of new operating spend)

Departments that demonstrate efficiency improvements in FY2025 will receive priority consideration for incremental budget in FY2026.

3. HEADCOUNT PLANNING

3.1 Headcount Request Process
All new headcount requests must include:
  - Business justification: revenue impact or cost savings supported by data
  - Role level: specify whether the request is for IC levels 1-6 or management levels M1-M3
  - Start quarter: Q1 (Apr-Jun), Q2 (Jul-Sep), Q3 (Oct-Dec 2026), or Q4 (Jan-Mar 2027)
  - Salary band: use the approved FY2026 salary bands provided by People Operations

3.2 Fully-Loaded Cost Assumptions
When calculating headcount costs, use these fully-loaded multipliers:
  - Canada-based hybrid employees: base salary x 1.32 (benefits, equipment, software, office space)
  - Canada-based remote employees: base salary x 1.26 (benefits, equipment, software)
  - Quebec-based employees: base salary x 1.34 (provincial payroll and benefit administration)

3.3 Approved Headcount by Department (FY2026 Allocation)
  - Engineering: 18 new positions (backend: 8, frontend: 4, DevOps/ML: 6)
  - Sales and Business Development: 5 new positions
  - Customer Success: 4 new positions
  - Product and Design: 3 new positions
  - People Operations: 2 new positions
  - Finance and Legal: 2 new positions
  - Marketing: 2 new positions
  - Leadership and Admin: 1 new position

Total approved positions: 37 (additional positions require CEO approval and are not guaranteed)

4. TECHNOLOGY AND INFRASTRUCTURE BUDGET

4.1 Cloud Infrastructure
FY2026 cloud infrastructure budget: CAD 900,000 (distributed across AWS, database services, and the LLM gateway).

Budget allocation by service:
  - AWS and supporting services: CAD 390,000
  - Database, vector storage, and backups: CAD 225,000
  - LLM gateway and model-provider costs: CAD 220,000
  - CDN, monitoring, and miscellaneous: CAD 65,000

Departments must submit infrastructure requests with projected usage metrics. Unforecasted overages above 15% of the approved budget require Finance approval.

4.2 Software and SaaS Tools
Total SaaS tools budget: CAD 125,000.
Approved tools for FY2026:
  - GitHub Enterprise: CAD 18,000
  - Jira and Confluence: CAD 13,000
  - Slack Pro: CAD 10,000
  - Password manager: CAD 5,000
  - Incident management: CAD 8,000
  - Design and research tools: CAD 9,000
  - Remaining discretionary tools: CAD 62,000

New SaaS subscriptions above CAD 750 per month require Finance and IT approval before purchase.

5. TRAVEL AND EVENTS BUDGET

FY2026 travel budget by department:
  - Sales: CAD 210,000 (client visits, trade shows)
  - Engineering: CAD 60,000 (conferences, on-site collaboration)
  - Product: CAD 40,000 (customer research visits, conferences)
  - All other departments: CAD 35,000 combined

Company-wide events:
  - Annual company offsite (all hands): CAD 150,000 (planned Q2 2026, Montreal)
  - Team quarterly offsites: CAD 7,500 per team per quarter

6. BUDGET SUBMISSION DEADLINES

15 January: People Operations distributes salary bands and benefits cost assumptions
31 January: Department heads submit draft headcount plans to People Operations
15 February: Full budget submissions due in Finance portal
28 February: Finance consolidates and presents board-ready budget
15 March: Board approval of FY2026 budget
01 April: FY2026 budget effective
